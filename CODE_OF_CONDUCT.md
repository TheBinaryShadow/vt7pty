# VT7Pty Code of Conduct

I want VT7Pty's project spaces to be welcoming and useful to people with
different experience, languages, hardware and reasons for using Windows 7.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical ideas and evidence respectfully.
- Be patient with questions and good-faith mistakes.
- Give constructive criticism and accept it without personal attacks.
- Respect other people's privacy and boundaries.
- Credit others' work and represent your own contributions honestly.
- Keep discussions relevant and respect my decisions about project scope.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, stalking, sexualized or unwanted attention,
personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information
without consent are not acceptable. Retaliation against someone who raises a
good-faith concern is also unacceptable.

## Reporting a concern

Email [r@binaryshadow.hr](mailto:r@binaryshadow.hr) with the subject
`VT7Pty conduct report`. Include relevant links, what happened, and how you
would prefer to be contacted. Do not post sensitive details in public issues.

I'll handle reports as privately as practical. I may share information with
people needed to investigate or respond, so I cannot promise absolute
confidentiality or a fixed response time.

Software vulnerabilities belong in the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

I may request a change in behavior, issue a warning, remove content, or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation. I'll consider severity,
context, repeated behavior and the safety of those involved.
Serious abuse may warrant immediate restriction.

Participants may request clarification or reconsideration through the reporting
email. Disagreement with a technical decision is welcome when expressed
respectfully; it does not justify harassment or disruption.

## Scope

This policy applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, project
discussions, and other spaces where someone represents VT7Pty.
