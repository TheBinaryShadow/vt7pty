## Why the keyboard test appeared broken

I received four failed US-keyboard attempts: one each from NESSY and LEOPARD,
and two from TURTLE. Each stops at exact manual line; Croatian input and
lock/unlock checks never start.

The input was received. For example, LEOPARD recorded AZaz followed by Enter,
and the fixture returned exactly AZaz with CRLF. NESSY's recorded letters also
match its backend read. TURTLE's first attempt shows the effect of Backspace,
and its second preserves the Unicode characters it received.

I caused the missing feedback in the test harness. ManualConsole disables local
echo to capture raw key records. The bridge forwards those keys into VT7Pty, but
Session::pump only updates its internal terminal model; the manual UI never
renders that output or a typed-input preview. Typing therefore appears to do
nothing even while it reaches the backend.

The US attempts also show mixed selected layouts: NESSY and LEOPARD record
Croatian, and TURTLE records US and Croatian in its two attempts. The first check
requires US. The corrected runner needs visible input feedback, clear current
and required layout information, and the actual backend readback on completion.

I keep all four attempts failed and all physical keyboard checks unqualified.
The data establishes receipt of the attempted input; it does not establish the
required US/Croatian workflow.
