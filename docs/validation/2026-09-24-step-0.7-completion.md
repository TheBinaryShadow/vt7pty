## Accepted environment disposition

The Non-ESU record reports machine name `NESSY`, manufacturer `VMware, Inc.`,
and model `VMware20,1`. That model normally identifies a virtualized guest and
therefore does not independently satisfy the project's ordinary physical-host
wording. The project owner previously directed that `NESSY` be treated as a
full-fledged physical machine and confirmed that the two Step 0.7 runs are the
successful results from both test machines.

This disposition is recorded rather than silently describing the
VMware-reported environment as native hardware. It applies to this Step 0.7
result. Later milestone and release gates continue to use the documented
environment requirement unless another explicit scope decision changes it.

## Decision

Step 0.7 is accepted. The exact candidate passed its development-host gates and
all 15 maintained cases on both accepted Windows 7 tiers. Its diagnostic
transport is bounded, machine-readable, versioned, attributable to the exact
build, and collectable without enabling input capture. The reviewed executable
lookup, command-line construction, IPC access control, temporary-file,
conversion, buffer, cancellation, and shutdown boundaries have no open Step
0.7 blocker.

The permanent test system proceeds in Step 0.8.
