## Cursor fix

I query cursor visibility from the buffer the scraper reads. I position
the VT cursor even while it is hidden and carry its last visibility
through screen clears. On WOLF the fixed build emits `CSI ?25 l` for the
alternate frame at (7, 23) and `CSI ?25 h` when CMD restores the primary
buffer at (0, 8). The two controls still show the alternate cursor.
