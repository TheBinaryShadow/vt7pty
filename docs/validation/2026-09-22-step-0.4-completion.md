## Accepted evidence deviation

The ESU and Non-ESU labels were produced in consecutive runs on the same
physical `TURTLE` Windows installation. The machine, OS identity, and hotfix
inventory are identical, so the records do not independently demonstrate two
different update states. The project owner explicitly directed that both
declared-tier runs be treated as passed for Step 0.4. This deviation is
recorded rather than obscured.

Future milestone and release acceptance retains the documented requirement to
use the intended physical tiers unless another explicit scope decision changes
that requirement.

## Decision

Step 0.4 is accepted. The XP/Vista background-desktop workaround and public
flag remain removed, the documented Windows 7 font and security APIs are the
active minimum paths, later-Windows branches remain intact, and the exact
candidate completed the accepted physical procedure without a test failure.

Technical rebranding and the `0.5.0-dev` identity now proceed in Step 0.5.
