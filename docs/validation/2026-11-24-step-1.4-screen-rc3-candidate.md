## What I changed

- **Direct-buffer resize.** I keep selection out of both growth and shrink
  on the detected modern console path. I resample geometry and cursor
  visibility after the resize before scraping the direct buffer. Windows 7
  continues to use its selection-based freeze.
- **Viewport.** I record native geometry before repaint and compare the
  stable frame at the offset measured from its initial origin. I require
  the native rows outside the comparison to be blank. The fixed-row
  control also checks that its marker stays at its original buffer location.
- **Live restoration.** I check the primary marker at its original location
  or at the measured full-width reflow location. I require the complete
  restored frame to match the native snapshot. A reflowed prompt may start
  after leading blanks, with the cursor at the end of the prompt.
